What the workflow estimates and what has been tested

The package applies the models from the published multisensor LFMC study. For each eligible satellite location, it prepares reflectance, weather, terrain and seasonal predictors. Annual land cover selects the grass, shrub or tree model. The package puts the predictors in the order expected by that trained Random Forest, applies its stored scaling, and converts the model output back to LFMC as percent dry mass.

Reflectance and vegetation models

Product-specific screening removes missing or unsuitable optical observations. Reflectance is kept at the product's native support. Sentinel-2 uses B8A and B11 at 20 m and averages red B4 from 10 m. MODIS and VIIRS keep their actual sinusoidal spacing. Sentinel-3 retains its per-pixel geolocation.

Before calculating spectral indices, each vegetation model applies its own stored calibration for the selected non-reference sensor and spacecraft. Sentinel-2 is the reference product and follows its reference preprocessing. The package does not fit new calibration coefficients or train a new forest during inference. Missing calibration domains stop the run.

The indices, seasonal terms, ordered predictors, scaler and inverse target transformation follow the stored published bundles. Predictions require all of a model's inputs to be finite. LFMC outputs are not clipped to an arbitrary percentage range.

Weather and optional elevation adjustment

AgERA5 version 2 supplies daily weather on its native 0.1-degree grid. Its daily labels follow the provider's local-day convention. The package associates those labels with the configured analysis date, preserving the study's date convention; it does not reconstruct hourly weather at satellite overpass time. See the provider description. Predictors include daily conditions and rainfall totals over the preceding 3, 7, 28 and 84 days. Those antecedent totals exclude the analysis day; the daily rainfall predictor uses that day itself. Missing required history is not replaced by zero.

By default, weather is interpolated to the satellite centres without the appendix elevation adjustment. Setting downscale: true applies the appendix constant-relative-humidity lapse methodology using local AW3D30 elevation and a reconstructed reference for the weather grid. That reference comes from a fixed historical field of the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts' high-resolution (HRES) model. The reference is explicitly approximate: equivalence to the weather provider's exact terrain and requested date/cycle has not been verified. Both modes retain separate provenance.

Terrain derivatives are calculated on the AW3D30 source grid before sampling. The optional adjustment changes weather predictors; it does not increase the resolution of the optical observation or establish a measured accuracy gain.

Land cover and quality screening

Direct workflows use WorldCover 2021 by default, with 2020 selectable. The map year is independent of the acquisition year and remains fixed across a time series. Changes in land cover after that snapshot are not reconstructed. The dominant category is selected among all map classes; only tree, shrub and grass categories route to a model. Training-site neighbourhood and suitability filters are not imposed on arbitrary inference locations.

Sentinel-3 aggregates class fractions over nominal 300 m central rectangles in local UTM coordinates. Its audited metadata-based QA excludes cloud, snow and specified retrieval failures. This screening is stricter than the older appendix example's mask. The historical replay preserves its original mask; it is not a claim that the current direct QA reproduces every historical pixel. Historical Dynamic World probabilities are not acquired by these direct workflows.

Evidence available for this candidate

Three checks answer different questions:

  1. Saved-case replay: can extracted inference reproduce the saved appendix outputs and masks for the checked Sentinel-2 and native Sentinel-3 cases?
  2. Real-input reference comparison: do newly prepared inputs produce the same predictors and predictions through the package and the trusted research helpers?
  3. Installed-package exercise: does a built, non-editable package work outside the research source tree, with the recorded environment and provider access?

The exercised Sardinia examples include all eight sensor/spacecraft paths in both weather modes. Their original and adjusted runs produced these valid-pixel counts:

Example Acquisition or analysis date Valid pixels in each mode
Sentinel-2 2021-07-22 8,701
Landsat 8 2021-07-15 3,047
Landsat 9 2021-11-18 3,047
Terra MODIS 2021-07-22, composite 20-27 July 16
Aqua MODIS 2021-07-22, same composite 6
Suomi NPP VIIRS 2021-07-22, same composite 16
Sentinel-3A 2021-07-22 42
Sentinel-3B 2021-07-21 49

The combined Windows check matched the baseline LFMC values and valid masks for all eight paths in both weather modes. Each original-mode path started with an empty provider cache. The downscaled paths reused the same checksum-verified historical weather reference. Later checks used cached inputs to verify polygon masking, the GUI and date-range processing.

Reproducing software outputs is distinct from validating LFMC against independent field measurements. The small examples do not establish general geographic/date coverage, every processing baseline or operational real-time availability. Linux/macOS, additional land-cover maps, mosaics, and scientific accuracy of the optional weather adjustment remain outside the demonstrated scope.
